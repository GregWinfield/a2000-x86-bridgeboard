# Sheet 4: static timing with the host link (owner requirement 8)

**Revision A, 2026-11-19.** Every Zorro, SRAM and clock figure is re-derived here with the complete design: the real SPI slave and protocol engine (`z2_hostif`) replaced the stub, and RP_SCK moved to pin 5 (S4-3). No figure from an earlier sheet is assumed to remain valid.

**Tool and command:** Lattice Diamond 3.14.0.75.2 (free licence), on the owner's PC.
- `bash hdl/syn/run_diamond.sh <work dir> <device>`; set `Z2_TOP=fit_top` for the fit test.
- The early-launch build (MISO launched on SCK rising, the 20 MHz option) sets the default of `Z2_LAUNCH_RISE` to 1 in the work copy of `z2_top.v`. Every other step is the same.

**Pins and constraints:** the committed [../../hdl/syn/z2_top.lpf](../../hdl/syn/z2_top.lpf).
- The LOCATE block is generated by `tools/fpga/pinplan.py` and frozen against G-SHEET2. The only exception is the authorised S4-3 swap: RP_SCK on pin 5 (PCLKT5_0), RP_IRQ_N on pin 1.
- New for sheet 4:
  - `FREQUENCY PORT "RP_SCK" 20 MHz`;
  - `BLOCK INTERCLOCKDOMAIN PATHS` (every SCK ↔ clk_core crossing is designed: Gray FIFO pointers, two-flop CS_N synchroniser, response data held static by the protocol; [sheet4-design-notes.md](sheet4-design-notes.md) §2.3);
  - `INPUT_SETUP` for RP_MOSI;
  - `CLOCK_TO_OUT … MAX 20 ns MIN 0 ns` for RP_MISO, measured from the launching SCK edge;
  - `PULLMODE=NONE` on the six host-link pins.

> **What this closes and what it does not.**
> - **Closes:** the static-timing part of owner requirement 8 for this revision. Every Zorro path, the SRAM and local-bus outputs, the 50 MHz core and the new SCK domain meet their constraints at both grades, at the frozen pins, on the LCMXO2-4000HC.
> - **Does not close:**
>   - **C-30** (approved 2026-11-19): the design no longer fits the LCMXO2-2000HC (§5), so the 4000HC is the Rev 1 device;
>   - the board-level SPI timing, which depends on assumptions about the ribbon and the RP2040 ([sheet4-timing-budget.md](sheet4-timing-budget.md), M-15);
>   - every hardware measurement (M-1 to M-16) and every later re-run after an RTL or pin change.

> **Rounding rule (owner refinement 4):** delays and requirements are rounded **up**; slacks, margins and minimum delays are rounded **down**.

## 1. Conditions

As [sheet2-sta-results.md](sheet2-sta-results.md) §1:
- −4 speed grade, commercial (TJ 85 °C) and industrial (TJ 100 °C);
- 15 pF on every output;
- every port LVCMOS33;
- both edges analysed;
- minimum delays from the fast model (hold analysis with `MIN 0 ns`).

**Pad check:** passed on every run (every port LVCMOS33, every used bank at VCCIO 3.3 V).

**Runs:** six on the LCMXO2-4000HC-4TG144:
- z2_top at C and I;
- the pin-budget fit test (101 signals) at C and I;
- the early-launch build of z2_top at C and I.

## 5. LCMXO2-2000HC: the design does not fit (C-30)

| Run | Slices | LUT4 | Registers | EBR | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| z2_top, 2000HC C (final RTL) | **1090 of 1056 (103 %)** | 2150 of 2112 (102 %) | 974 of 2448 | 2 of 8 | `ERROR - map: Design doesn't fit into device specified` |
| z2_top, 4000HC C | 1090 of 2160 (50 %) | 2150 of 4320 (50 %) | 974 of 4665 | 2 of 10 | Fits; PIO 90 + 4 JTAG of 115 |
| fit test, 4000HC C | 1085 of 2160 (50 %) | 2145 of 4320 (50 %) | 982 of 4665 | 2 of 10 | Fits; PIO 101 + 4 JTAG of 115 |

**What did not help:** the area rework earlier in this sheet reduced the first attempt (1177 slices, 111 %) to 1089:
- one response-CRC instance instead of six;
- precomputed frame end positions;
- the staging and response buffers in EBR (2 × DP8KC, 512 × 8).

The host-link engine itself is about 461 FF / 866 LUT4 (Diamond area report). Fitting the 2000HC would need a smaller protocol engine (fewer commands, no ECHO, a smaller staging buffer) and gives no margin for sheets 5–6.

**C-30, approved by the owner 2026-11-19:** the **LCMXO2-4000HC-4TG144C** is the Rev 1 device.
- It is the documented migration path of D-02.
- It is pin-compatible at every frozen pin; the sheet-2 migration check passes against the 4000HC pinout extract.
- At ~50 % it leaves room for sheets 5–6.

Price and availability: TBV before BOM freeze ([../bom/bom-preliminary.csv](../bom/bom-preliminary.csv)).

## 6. R-43: the 50 MHz margin during this sheet

| Step | Worst 50 MHz slack | Cause / change |
|---|---|---|
| Sheet 2 rev A1, 4000HC (stub host port) | 3.859 ns | – |
| First 4000HC run with `z2_hostif` | **−2.245 ns** (timing errors) | Engine decode, read data and status paths. Fixed: argument check pipelined (X_PRE/X_PRE2), read data registered at `h_ack`, status inputs registered |
| After those fixes | **0.731 ns** (below the 1 ns trigger) | The host side of `z2_mbx` was live for the first time: the stub had tied it off, so its range checks were never synthesised. Critical path: Q0_CONS → producer range check → event flag |
| Producer check: parallel subtractions | 0.741 ns | Paths moved to the QCTL link-reset decode (16-bit magic compare → flush, fan-out 25) |
| Queue flags as equality compares; QCTL magic decoded at the source (`zr_magic`, `h_magic`) | 1.488 ns | Remaining worst path in the host parser: `pb < p_end7` |
| `p_lt7` register (final) | **2.216 ns** | Spread over z2_mbx, z2_core and z2_hostif paths |

**Every change keeps the behaviour exactly:**
- Two rewrites are identical whenever invariant M1 holds. M1 is formally proven, and an exhaustive check covers every queue depth.
- The others are registers whose values the formal properties M6/M7, or a simulation check at every clock, compare with the original expressions.
- Mutants of each are detected ([sheet4-design-notes.md](sheet4-design-notes.md) §5.2).

**Lesson for requirement 8:** a stub that ties off a port also removes the logic behind it from timing. The host-side mailbox paths were never analysed until the real host interface existed.

## 7. Revision A1 (2026-11-19): no re-run needed, one confirming run

**Unchanged since the six runs above:**
- RTL, LPF and pins (`pinplan.py` on the 4000HC data produces byte-identical LOCATEs);
- the device.

The S4-6 circuit (rev A1, and its rev A2 revision: TLV3201, Q401/Q402, R424 removed) and the sheet-2 symbol change (C-30) are board-side and do not enter the FPGA timing.
