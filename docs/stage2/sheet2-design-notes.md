## 3. Pin assignment

### 3.1 Method: one source of truth, frozen after approval

`tools/fpga/pinplan.py` holds the plan: each signal with its bank and, where a function demands it, a fixed pin. It reads the Lattice pin data and assigns the rest in pad order. From that one computation it writes:
- the LOCATE block in `hdl/syn/z2_top.lpf`, plus `hdl/syn/fit_top_extra.lpf` for the 16 pins that exist only in the fit test until sheets 4–6 are drawn;
- the schematic pins: `tools/kicad/sheet2.py` calls the same function;
- the pin table [sheet2-pin-assignment.md](sheet2-pin-assignment.md).

**Freeze (revision A1).** The G-SHEET2-approved assignment is recorded in `tools/fpga/pins_gsheet2.csv`.
- `pinplan.py` fails if any pin differs, unless the change is listed in its `CHANGES` table with the owner decision.
- Pads freed on purpose are held in `KEEP_FREE`. At present that is pin 67, so the automatic assignment cannot reuse it.
- A mutation test showed the check catches both a pin moving and an undeclared removal.

**Audit.** `audit_sheet2.py` checks the exported netlist against three sources, independently of the generator:
- the pin data;
- the LOCATE lines Diamond actually used;
- the HDL port directions in `fit_top.v`.

The mutation test changed one thing at a time in a copy of the netlist, and once in the LPF. All 8 faults were detected:
1. two FPGA pins swapped;
2. a test point removed;
3. a second driver on an FPGA output;
4. a Zorro input left undriven;
5. a pull-up value changed;
6. the TCK pull-down moved to +3V3;
7. LED_READY and a DIP input swapped;
8. an LPF site changed.

## 4. Power and decoupling

- **Supplies** (S49 Table 3.2): the HC core takes VCC 2.375–3.6 V and VCCIO 1.14–3.6 V.
  - VCC (4 pins) and all 12 VCCIO pins are on **+3V3**. Note 1 of that table requires like supplies to be tied together.
  - Every bank is LVCMOS33. The pad report confirms VCCIO 3.3 V in each bank on every run.
- **Decoupling** (S64 Table 3.1):
  - VCC: 2 × 10 µF + 100 nF per pin (C201–C204, C217–C218);
  - VCCIO: 10 µF per bank + 100 nF per pin (C205–C216, C219–C224);
  - 100 nF parts are 0603 X7R 25 V; 10 µF parts are 0805 X5R 16 V;
  - placement at each pin is a layout requirement (S64 §15).
- **Carried to sheet 5** (+3V3):
  - accuracy within 3 % (S64 §2.2);
  - monotonic ramp of 0.01–100 V/ms (S49 Table 3.3);
  - POR needs VCC > 2.1 V and VCCIO0 > 1.06 V.

## 8. Fail-safe behaviour with an erased or unconfigured FPGA (refinement 5)

MachXO2 I/O are tri-stated with a weak pull-down (30–305 µA) before and during configuration, and in an erased device [V S49]. Sheet 1 rev B was designed so that every default resistor leaves the card **inactive and non-driving** in that state, with the AutoConfig chain passing ([sheet1-design-notes.md](sheet1-design-notes.md): card-enable and AutoConfig bypass; C-10 closed by design). Sheet 2 changes none of those resistors; the audit checks every ZF net's members.

| FPGA pin (state: weak pull-down) | External default | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ZF_SLAVE_N, ZF_CFGOUT_N, ZF_XRDY, ZF_INT2_N, ZF_INT6_N, ZF_DBUF_OE_N | 2.2 kΩ pull-ups (R101–R105, R108) to +3V3 | Negated | Netlist |
| ZF_ARM | R122 10 kΩ pull-down | **Not armed**, so CARD_EN_N is high: buffers off, XRDY/INTx gated, the slot looks empty, and /CFGOUTn passes | Sheet 1 rev B |
| ZF_DBUF_DIR | R109 10 kΩ pull-down | Defined direction; the buffer is disabled anyway | Netlist |
| SRAM_CE_N, SRAM_WE_N | R301/R302 2.2 kΩ pull-ups | SRAM deselected, no write | Sheet 3 |
| SA0–SA16, SRAM_OE_N/UB_N/LB_N | Weak pull-down only | Defined levels; no effect while deselected | – |
| SA17 (SN) | R208 4.7 kΩ pull-up | High; also keeps SN high so the SSPI port stays inactive (§3.3) | S64 |
| LD0–LD15 | U103 bus-hold | Not driven by anyone; levels held | Sheet 1 |
| LED_READY | Weak pull-down; LED driver on sheet 6 | Off = "not armed or FPGA blank" (READY code) | – |
| I2C pins 125/126 | Sheet-5 pull-ups | The erased-device configuration port ACKs 0x40–0x43 (C-28) | TN-02155 §6 |
| **RP_IRQ_N, RP_ATTN, RP_MISO (sheet 4); X4_PWR_EN (sheet 5); DBG0–3 (sheet 6)** | **None yet** | **Requirement for sheets 4–6:** each needs a default resistor that sets its safe level against the weak pull-down: RP_IRQ_N must not read as asserted, and X4_PWR_EN low must mean X4 power off. Each sheet must also check the 30–305 µA range | Carried (§9) |

**New hardware check M-14.** M-14 extends M-6 (power-up/down with an unprogrammed FPGA). The FPGA is tested in three states:
- erased (factory blank);
- during configuration, at power-up and on a PROGRAMN pulse at TP201;
- after a failed configuration (INITN low or DONE low).

**Pass criteria in all three states:**
- no /SLAVEn, /INTx or XRDY assertion;
- no data-buffer enable;
- /CFGOUTn passes /CFGIN to the next slot;
- SRAM /CE high (TP301);
- READY LED off;
- no contention on LD (bus-hold levels only);
- an I2C scan of the telemetry bus shows only the expected devices, plus the FPGA configuration port at 0x40–0x43 while the device is erased (C-28).

**Also:** READY must show the card state with the X4 absent or unpowered (C-26).
